# The trilemma registry

**The trilemma** (papers 2 and 4 of this program): a theory cannot
simultaneously have (i) coupled ghost sectors, (ii) empirical Lorentz
invariance, and (iii) definite configurations (a single-world sample
space). Any two, never all three. Horn 1 = keep ghosts +
configurations, pay with a Lorentz-violating frame. Horn 2 = keep
Lorentz + configurations, pay by decoupling/removing the ghost.
Horn 3 = keep ghosts + Lorentz, pay by abandoning definite
configurations.

**Standing challenge:** submit a fourth position — a published
escape route that keeps all three, or that does not reduce to one of
the horns (or to disputing the rate premise). Open an issue or write
to the author. Every submission gets a dated entry and an honest
adjudication.

This registry is repo infrastructure, not a publication. Entries
below the line are seeded from paper 4's Table 1 (audited); entries
marked PENDING have not yet been carefully classified by this
program and carry no horn claim.

## Conventions note

The "conditional mass creation" mechanism named in the paper-4 and
paper-6 rows' source material (the k=2 indivisibility signature of
the conformal cell) is stated relative to the minimal
(sigma_max/contraction) survival-conditioning convention; divisor
NEGATIVITY is convention-free, the column-sum crossing is
convention-relative, and the minimal convention is justified as the
unique normalization that discards no survival probability beyond
what the dynamics itself loses. Definitions and the minimality
dichotomy: paper 6 Sec. 3 (conventions paragraph), paper 4 Sec. 4;
verified in `scripts/t4_jordan_process.py` [3b] and
`scripts/p6_divisor_gate.py` [4].
