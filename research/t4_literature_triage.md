## A1. Bender-Mannheim PRD 78, 025022 (2008), arXiv:0804.4190 - VERIFIED

Title: "Exactly solvable PT-symmetric Hamiltonian having no Hermitian counterpart."
Abstract verified by direct fetch. Exact claims (quoted from abstract):

- In the equal-frequency limit "the similarity transform that was used for the
  unequal-frequency case becomes singular and H_PU becomes a Jordan-block
  operator, which is nondiagonalizable and has fewer energy eigenstates than
  eigenvalues."
- "Such a Hamiltonian has no Hermitian counterpart. Thus, the equal-frequency
  PT theory emerges as a distinct realization of quantum mechanics."
- "the Hilbert space is complete with a set of nonstationary solutions to the
  Schroedinger equation replacing the missing stationary ones."
- "These nonstationary states are needed to establish that the Jordan-block
  Hamiltonian of the equal-frequency Pais-Uhlenbeck model generates unitary
  time evolution."

So the B-M position: equal-frequency PU = Jordan block = nondiagonalizable, no
Hermitian counterpart, completeness restored by nonstationary (t-dependent,
polynomially growing) solutions, and unitarity is CLAIMED to survive.

Companion: Bender-Mannheim PRL 100, 110402 (2008), arXiv:0706.0207 (verified):
"the prototypical fourth-order derivative Pais-Uhlenbeck oscillator model is
shown to be free of states of negative energy or negative norm. When correctly
formulated (as a PT symmetric theory), the theory determines its own Hilbert
space and associated positive-definite inner product. In this Hilbert space the
model is found to be a fully acceptable quantum-mechanical theory that exhibits
unitary time evolution." (This is the unequal-frequency no-ghost theorem; the
abstract does not mention gravity.) See also Bender-Mannheim "Giving up the
ghost," J. Phys. A 41, 304018 (2008) (arXiv:0807.2607 - ID from memory, flag
for double-check at citation time).

## A2. Critiques and follow-ups on the equal-frequency PU

(a) Smilga, "Comments on the dynamics of the Pais-Uhlenbeck oscillator,"
    SIGMA 5, 017 (2009), arXiv:0808.0139 - VERIFIED. Direct anti-claim:
    in the Bender-Mannheim nonstandard realization "wave functions grow
    exponentially for large real values of canonical coordinates"; "The free
    nonstandard PU oscillator is unitary when the frequencies are different";
    but "unitarity is broken in the equal frequencies limit." Also: unequal
    frequencies give a pure point spectrum dense everywhere; equal-frequency
    spectrum is continuous. THIS IS THE SHARPEST PUBLISHED CONTRADICTION of
    0804.4190: same object, opposite unitarity verdict.

(b) Smilga, "Exceptional points of infinite order give a continuous spectrum,"
    arXiv:1409.8450 - VERIFIED. The equal-frequency point is an exceptional
    point (EP) of INFINITE order; infinitely many eigenstates coalesce into
    Jordan blocks; the signature is a continuous spectrum; "an infinity of
    nontrivial states that do not change their norm during evolution appear."
    Positioning: Smilga treats the infinite Jordan blocks as signaling a
    continuum, not as a new unitary realization.

(c) Mostafazadeh, "A Hamiltonian formulation of the Pais-Uhlenbeck oscillator
    that yields a stable and unitary quantum system," arXiv:1008.4678 -
    VERIFIED. Unequal frequencies: genuinely Hermitian positive Hamiltonian.
    Degenerate (equal-frequency) case: "a consistent description requires
    analytic continuation into a complex Hamiltonian system possessing a
    generalized PT-symmetry" - i.e. even the friendliest alternative
    quantization concedes the equal-frequency point is qualitatively singular.
    Also Mostafazadeh, "Imaginary-scaling versus indefinite-metric quantization
    of the Pais-Uhlenbeck oscillator," arXiv:1107.1874 (PRD 84, 105018).

(d) Pavsic, "Some comments on ghosts and unitarity: the Pais-Uhlenbeck
    oscillator revisited," arXiv:1301.4879 (found in sweep; pro-stability
    camp for the interacting PU).

(e) 2020s activity - the EP/Jordan structure of resonant PU is still live:
    - Fring, Marquette, Taira, "Spectrum-generating algebra and intertwiners
      of the resonant Pais-Uhlenbeck oscillator," arXiv:2601.20752 (Jan 2026)
      - VERIFIED: at resonance "the dynamics becomes non-diagonalisable and
      the conventional Fock-space construction collapses"; they build
      intertwiners organizing generalized eigenvectors into finite Jordan
      chains, hidden su(2) only at resonance; and crucially "classically
      equivalent Hamiltonians define inequivalent quantum theories" (one
      non-diagonalizable, an alternative fully diagonalizable).
    - Conformal-bridge / PT work: arXiv:2104.08351 (conformal bridge and PT
      symmetry); arXiv:2602.12858 (conformal bi-Hamiltonian PU, 2026).
    So the equal-frequency PU quantization is STILL CONTESTED in 2026, and
    nobody adjudicates it with a stochastic-process criterion.

(f) 1/k^4 propagator <-> Jordan blocks per mode: this connection IS in print,
    but essentially only by Mannheim himself. Mannheim, "Ghost problems from
    Pauli-Villars to fourth-order quantum gravity and their resolution,"
    arXiv:2004.00376, and "Solution to the ghost problem in higher-derivative
    gravity," arXiv:2109.12743 (Nuovo Cim. C 45, 27) - VERIFIED: "For the pure
    1/k^4 propagator the Hamiltonian is non-diagonalizable," no negative-energy
    or negative-norm states, "instead there are states of zero norm that
    maintain probability conservation"; the M^2 -> 0 limit of the second-plus-
    fourth-order theory is singular. Nobody outside the Mannheim camp uses the
    per-mode exceptional-point language for conformal gravity's dipole/double-
    pole ("dipole ghost") propagator, and nobody gives it a measure-theoretic /
    stochastic reading.

## B4. Rates and bounds in quadratic gravity (Stelle/agravity)

(a) Cline, Jeon, Moore, "The phantom menaced: constraints on low-energy
    effective ghosts," PRD 70, 043543 (2004), hep-ph/0311312 - VERIFIED.
    The template: ghost + gravity => vacuum -> 2 ghost + 2 gamma; comparing to
    the cosmic gamma-ray background forces a LORENTZ-VIOLATING UV cutoff
    <~ 3 MeV, and "theories of ghosts with a Lorentz-conserving cutoff are
    completely excluded." (Our empirical-LI horn anchor.)

(b) Gross, Strumia, Teresi, Zirilli, "Is negative kinetic energy meta-stable?"
    PRD 103, 115025 (2021), arXiv:2007.05541 - VERIFIED, THE KEY PRIOR ART.
    - They DO treat the gravitational case explicitly: they "analytically and
      numerically compute the run-away rate finding that it is cosmologically
      slow in 4-derivative gravity, where ghosts have gravitational
      interactions only" (abstract). Mechanism: Planck-suppressed couplings.
    - But at the QFT level: "In quantum field theory the ghost run-away rate
      is naively infinite in perturbation theory"; the Lorentz-invariant vacuum
      gives "an integral over the non-compact Lorentz group"; and they state
      plainly they "do not know the true rate."
    - Net: classical/semiclassical metastability claim for agravity, with an
      explicitly acknowledged open QFT divergence traced to Lorentz invariance.
      This is simultaneously our biggest preemption risk (a computed gravity
      rate exists) and our best setup (the divergence they cannot cure is
      exactly what the indivisible-stochastic exit/explosion classification is
      built to formalize).

(c) Salvio, Strumia, "Agravity," JHEP 06 (2014) 080, arXiv:1403.4226 -
    dimensionless quadratic gravity program; ghost issue deferred.
    Salvio, Strumia, "Quantum mechanics of 4-derivative theories," EPJC 76,
    227 (2016), arXiv:1512.01237 - VERIFIED: 4-derivative dof quantized with
    unusual time-inversion parity representation; "positive energy eigenvalues,
    normalizable wave functions, and unitary evolution in a negative-norm

## B5. Conformal gravity specifics

- Pure Weyl gravity: dimensionless alpha_g, massless k^4 graviton sector, no
  separate ghost mass scale. NO published computation of a vacuum-decay /
  instability RATE for conformal gravity's ghost sector coupled to matter was
  found (searched: "conformal gravity vacuum decay rate," "Weyl gravity ghost
  decay," "alpha_g bound instability," CJM-analog searches). GSTZ 2007.05541
  covers 4-derivative gravity WITH the Einstein term (massive ghost, Planck
  suppression); their suppression argument does not port to the scale-free
  Weyl case, where the ghost-matter coupling is alpha_g-controlled and
  massless. This is a genuinely open computation.
- Closest single paper: Deruelle, Sasaki, Sendouda, Youssef, "Lorentz-
  violating vs ghost gravitons: the example of Weyl gravity," JHEP 09 (2012)
  009, arXiv:1202.3131 - VERIFIED: eliminating the Weyl ghost by local
  Lorentz-symmetry breaking forces a vacuum redefinition whose primordial GW
  spectrum "blows up when the Lorentz-violating scale becomes comparable to
  the Hubble radius, in sharp contrast to standard Weyl gravity where the
  gravitational ghosts smoothly damp out the spectrum." I.e., in the ONE place
  someone traded Weyl ghosts for Lorentz violation, the trade is observable -
  a mini two-horn version of our trilemma, in cosmology.
- Strongest counter-arguments in print to Mannheim's ghost-free claim:
  1. Smilga arXiv:0808.0139 / 1409.8450: unitarity of the B-M realization
     FAILS precisely in the equal-frequency (= conformal) limit; EP of
     infinite order -> continuous spectrum, not a healthy Fock space.
  2. Hell, Lust, Zoupanos, "On the ghost problem of conformal gravity," JHEP
     08 (2023) 168, arXiv:2306.13714 - VERIFIED: "confirm the presence of
     ghosts" around dS and Minkowski; Maldacena's Neumann + positive-frequency
     boundary conditions do NOT yield a general solution; agreement with
     Einstein gravity requires additional/alternative conditions.
  3. Ilhan, Kovner, "Conformal gravity redux: ghost-turned-tachyon,"
     arXiv:1403.4664 (PRD 89) - VERIFIED abstract: the perturbative ghost
     mode "turns into a tachyon when nonlinear interaction is accounted for";
     "solutions of nonlinear classical equations of motion develop a
     singularity in finite time" (a Feller-explosion-shaped classical result!).
  4. Salvio-Strumia 1512.01237 implicitly: their positive-energy quantization
     lives in a negative-norm configuration space - i.e. even the friendly
     quantization concedes the Born-rule sample space is lost (they then build
     a generic-norm formalism).
  5. Kuntz, "Unitarity through PT symmetry in quantum quadratic gravity,"
     CQG 42, 175003 (2025), arXiv:2410.08278 - pro-PT but notably concedes
     quadratic gravity "generally violates unitarity when viewed as a
     Hermitian QFT"; evidence-level ("compelling evidence"), not theorem.
  6. Buoninfante 2501.04097 / 2606.18349: first-sheet complex-conjugate poles
     distinguish true ghosts from unstable particles - undercuts both the
     "it's just an unstable resonance" and (indirectly) zero-norm escapes at
     the level of the dressed propagator.
