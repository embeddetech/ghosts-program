Cross-paper meta-research registry, companion to `auditor-error-log.md`
(which logs the *rejected* leads). This file logs the *confirmed*
CRITICAL/MAJOR findings — and any finding that REVERSED a prior
maintainer fix — recording **how deep each one survived** before an
audit lane caught it. The point of the release gate is to make
"audited" measurable; this file measures the gate's own catch profile
(what slips, how far, and which model/framing finally catches it).

Per-paper `histories/paperN(-gate)-history.md` carry every pass in
context; this is the cross-paper index of the *serious* catches.

## What to log here
- Any CRITICAL or MAJOR finding (per the audit lane's severity), once
  VERIFIED under rule 7 and applied.
- Any finding, at any severity, that REVERSED or corrected a prior
  maintainer fix (prior-fix errors are high-value meta-data even when
  minor).
- Not MINOR/NIT that merely tightened wording with no prior-fix
  reversal (those live in the per-paper ledgers only).

## Survival depth (the headline metric)
**Survival depth = the number of prior COMPLETED audit passes whose
relevant lane examined the same (materially unchanged) text and did
NOT flag it.** Annotate with: whether the text was PUBLISHED and/or
externally circulated while the defect was latent; how many of those
prior passes were declared CLEAN in that lane; and the models that
missed it. For a defect INTRODUCED by a later edit and caught soon
after, depth is small (≈ passes since introduction) — tag it
`prior-fix-error` so it isn't confused with a long-latent survivor.

## Failure-mode classes (extend as needed; mirror release-gate rule 6)
FALSE-CONVERSE · HALF-DOMAIN-SCAN-GENERALIZED · OVERCLAIM (strength
miscalibration) · EPSILON-AS-EXACT · PRIOR-ECHO-MC · FIX-PROPAGATION ·
PRIOR-FIX-ERROR · CITATION/THEOREM-NUMBER · SYMBOL/NOTATION ·
PRIOR-ART-MISS · SCOPE (hypothesis/quantifier).

  historical Achilles' heel; the abstract/intro "exactly/precisely the
  condition" phrasings are the recurring host.
- **Prior-fix errors happen** (3, 7): a fix applied in one pass can be
  wrong and is caught 1 pass later, sometimes cross-model. Re-verify the
  *site of every prior fix*, not just fresh text.
- **Deepest survivors are OVERCLAIMS and FALSE-CONVERSES in
  abstract/title prose** (4 at depth 8; 6 survived ledgered-clean
  claims lanes + publication), not calculational errors — every
  theorem has re-derived clean on every pass. The gate's blind spot is
  calibration/scope of stated claims (prose AND numerics), not math.

  pass flagged the alpha_g window as MINOR and an Opus claims pass the
  *next* round independently escalated the SAME site to MAJOR — the
  two-pass-per-paper cadence turned a soft flag into a corroborated
  serious finding. Running the second pass before the author adjudicates
  is what produced the corroboration (do not "resolve" a held MINOR by
  applying it before the confirming pass runs).
- **Held-for-author MAJORs are logged too** (11, 12): a MAJOR verified by
  the orchestrator (and/or cross-model) but whose *fix flavor* is an
  author decision is recorded here at catch time, resolution = HELD, so
  survival-depth data isn't lost waiting on adjudication.
- **"Generalize beyond what was measured" spans lanes** (3, 6, 8): a
  quantifier over an unscanned domain (claims) and a sampled statistic
  asserted as a trajectory-wide bound (numerics, entry 8) are the same
  disease — caught only by re-running over the FULL domain / trajectory.
  The fix is always to scope the statement to what was actually computed.
