## Disposition taxonomy
- **applied** — fix made to the manuscript this pass (under the sibling-sweep gate).
- **held-defensible** — verified defensible, intentionally NOT changed (also in auditor-error-log).
- **held-biblio** — a citation/quote/eq-number item parked for the biblio / pre-circulation pass (often [[pdf-reads-provisional]]).
- **deferred-notation** — a symbol-collision parked for a dedicated notation micro-pass.
- **flagged-numerics** — root-caused, parked for the paper's numerics pass (no hasty number edit).
- **reported** — noted, no action needed (deliberate convention, or readability-only, or honest downstream hedge).
